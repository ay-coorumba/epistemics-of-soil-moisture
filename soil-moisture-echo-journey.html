Two echoes, two contracts

The passive journey ends in an absolute number with a physics pedigree and a 0.04 m³/m³ error budget. The active journeys end differently: ASCAT delivers a relative number — beautifully stable, near-daily, but only meaningful against its own archive — and Sentinel-1 delivers resolution nobody else can touch, paid for in speckle and retrieval immaturity. None of the three is "the best"; they fail in different directions, which is exactly why the long-term climate records merge all of them.

Read them together: the glow's journey for how a measurement becomes an inference, and this one for how an echo becomes two entirely different products.

Worked for ASCAT (MetOp, C-band scatterometer, TU Wien change detection) and Sentinel-1 (C-band SAR). Equations are the standard published forms (radar equation, Water Cloud model, σ⁰ normalisation, Kp, speckle statistics, change detection); worked numbers and per-step σ values are illustrative magnitudes, not operational algorithm internals.
