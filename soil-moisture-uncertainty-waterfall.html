A budget with no slack

Each step adds one error source in quadrature. Hover a bar for its numbers. The whole budget adds up to essentially the entire 0.04 requirement — there is almost no margin, and vegetation alone is the biggest single piece.

Anchor value (unbiased RMSE ≤ 0.04 m³/m³) is the published SMAP mission requirement; per-source contributions are a representative decomposition for illustration — real magnitudes vary by land cover and study, and are worth a subject-matter check before publication. Systematic biases are separately removed by rescaling (CDF-matching to a reference climatology), so this is an unbiased RMSE; that rescaling borrows its own assumptions.
