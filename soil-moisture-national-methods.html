How nations gather soil-moisture data — and whose data becomes “the truth”

“Global soil moisture” isn’t one measurement. It’s a patchwork of national programmes that each chose a different instrument, philosophy, and openness policy — each described here in its own language. Compare the four approaches, then see how the global reference record actually gets assembled, and who gets left out.

How the global record actually gets assembled

The merged climate records scientists rely on are weighted toward whatever data is open and easy to integrate. Each nation’s share of the reference record diverges sharply from its share of on-orbit sensing capability — the gap is what openness policy, not physics, decides.
