Kaswari AgTechThe Epistemics of Soil Moisture

A satellite soil-moisture value looks like a measurement. It is really a skilled inference with a long pedigree — averaged over kilometres, corrected by models, drawn through black boxes, sampled once every few days, and “validated” against a reference that measures something else entirely. This is thirteen ways of looking at one number.

Each piece is a standalone, self-contained page — open any of them directly. The links here are relative, so keep the files together in one folder for navigation to work. Interactive scenes need a modern browser; the 3D pieces stream terrain and satellite imagery live and fall back gracefully offline.

On honesty: the collection’s numbers — error budgets, footprint sizes, national capability shares — are illustrative unless cited, chosen to convey the direction and structure of each effect rather than exact operational values. Mission facts and the retrieval physics are drawn from NASA / ESA / EUMETSAT / CAS sources and ~29 peer-reviewed papers retrieved via Consensus; the country-specific instrument details are the ones most worth re-verifying before publication. The point was never to be the operational algorithm — it was to make the epistemic life of a number visible.
