Make the invisible corrections visible — by breaking them

Every SMAP pixel is the output of a retrieval that quietly corrects for vegetation, roughness, interference and temperature. Those corrections are invisible when they work. Switch one off and watch the error appear exactly where the physics says it should — dry stripes over forests, a spike over the interference source. The left map is what you’d publish; the right map is how wrong it is.
